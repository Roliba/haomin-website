Biography & CVCV & Biography

Hao-Min HuangHao-Min Huang

Born in Taiwan in 1997, Hao-Min Huang is an artist working with interactive installations and multimedia. He graduated from the Institute of Technology and Art at National Tsing Hua University. Shaped by an interdisciplinary background and an ongoing exploration of new media art, his practice seeks new forms of expression at the intersection of technology and art. His works span creative coding, kinetic mechanisms, virtual reality and spatial installations, exploring the relationships between visual perception, bodily experience and spatial awareness, while extending these inquiries to the conditions of his own existence.

Influenced by the Maker Movement, he experiments by repurposing technological objects and juxtaposing diverse materials. This process involves both redesigning technology and reconsidering everyday things, revealing new narratives and perceptual experiences within existing technological frameworks. His installations go beyond mechanical movement or programmed control to question the relationships between people and technology, and between people and their environment.

He is particularly interested in exchanges between virtual and physical realities. Combining VR, kinetic sculpture and sound installations, he creates immersive experiences that invite audiences to enter unfamiliar spaces through bodily perception. His focus is on how technology shapes our understanding of ourselves and our surroundings, opening up further possibilities for imagination. Balancing precision and emotion, he uses a technical vocabulary to construct poetic artistic experiences that express his perspective and creative thinking.
