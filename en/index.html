Practice Practice

Born in Taiwan in 1997, Hao-Min Huang graduated from the Institute of Technology and Art at National Tsing Hua University. Working with interaction, installation and multimedia, he combines creative coding, virtual reality and kinetic devices to explore visual perception, bodily experience and spatial awareness, extending these questions to his own existence. He also experiments with incorporating installation into sound performance to develop alternative performance formats. Influenced by the Maker Movement, he repurposes technological objects and juxtaposes materials, reconsidering and redesigning familiar things to reveal new possibilities.
