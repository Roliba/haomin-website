HAO-MIN HUANG / SELECTED WORK

LapseSelected work

A participant draws a trace. Two devices repeatedly interpret, recognize and transmit it through light and cameras. The transformations that emerge through this cycle reveal the distortions inherent in communication between individuals.

Artists
Hao-Min Huang, Po-Yao Wu
Materials
Single-channel real-time generative imagery, mechanical devices
