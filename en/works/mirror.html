HAO-MIN HUANG / SELECTED WORK

Spatiotemporal MirrorSelected work

Long-exposure images record a space and are drawn layer by layer on a wall. Space and time are compressed into a plane, with the movements of viewers gradually becoming part of the work.

Artists
Po-Yao Wu, Hao-Min Huang
Materials
Multimedia, V-plotter, image processing
