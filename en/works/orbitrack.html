HAO-MIN HUANG / SELECTED WORK

OrbitrackSelected work

Orbits and traces intertwine in space. Light breathes, turns and fades across metal carriers, evoking the workings of an artificial universe. Each rotation marks a passage of time that cannot return to its origin.

Artists
Hao-Min Huang, Daylight Effect
Materials
Metal sheets, acrylic, brushes, stepper motors, LED modules, control circuitry
