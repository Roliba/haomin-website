HAO-MIN HUANG / SELECTED WORK

Free CompanionSelected work

A mobile device combines live images of its surroundings with virtual characters. Participants can pose and dance with these figures, exploring everyday encounters at the intersection of virtual and physical realities.

Artists
Hao-Min Huang, Po-Yao Wu
Materials
Augmented reality, 3D avatars, image processing
